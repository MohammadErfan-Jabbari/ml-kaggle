### Sensitivity Analysis

**PLSRegression**: Strong monotonic improvement
- n=5: 0.653 → n=7: 0.700 → n=10: 0.713 → n=15: 0.733 → n=20: 0.743
- Range: 0.0897 (highly sensitive)
- Trend: "Best at highest value, suggesting increasing performance"

**LGBImportanceSelector**: Insensitive to k
- k=250: 0.699 → k=500: 0.705 → k=750: 0.697 → k=1000: 0.698 → k=1500: 0.696
- Range: 0.0089 (very flat)
- Trend: "Very flat, method is insensitive to this hyperparameter"

**VarianceThreshold**: Peaked early
- t=0.001: 0.708 → t=0.005: 0.708 → t=0.01: 0.697 → t=0.02: 0.686 → t=0.05: 0.694
- Range: 0.0212
- Trend: "Peak at threshold=0.005, plateau beyond"

### Statistical Significance

None are statistically significant at α=0.05:
- PLS(n=20): p=0.073 (marginal)
- LGB(k=500): p=0.550
- Var(t=0.005): p=0.283

However, PLS(n=20) has the largest effect size (+4.85%).
