## Problem Statement

The pipeline was using **K.pneumoniae AUC** as the primary metric for model selection, but the Kaggle leaderboard scores **mean AUC across all 8 antibiotics**. This caused:
- Phase 5.5b: Optimized K.pn (+5.6%) → LB decreased (-1.6%)
- Best submission (LB 0.83862) was abandoned because it looked bad on K.pn
- Worst submission (LB 0.82451) was selected because it had good K.pn

## Key Findings

### 1. Model Selection Impacts

The metric change affects which models are selected:

| Phase | Old Best (K.pn) | New Best (Mean) | Δ Performance |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Phase 2 | pls_n20 (K.pn=0.7431) | var_t005 (Mean=0.9004) | Old was 1.5% worse on LB metric! |
| Phase 4 | 4B1 (K.pn=0.6697) | 4A1 (Mean=0.8842) | Rankings changed |
| Phase 5.5b | 5A2 (both metrics) | 5A2 (both metrics) | No change |

**Critical Discovery**: The old metric was selecting models that were **worse on the leaderboard**!

### 2. Validation vs Leaderboard Correlation

Analyzed correlation between metrics and actual LB performance:

| Metric Type | Gap to LB | Reliability | Example |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Val Mean AUC** | ~1% | ✅ Reliable | Val=0.8142 → LB=0.8245 |
| **OOF Mean AUC** | ~6.5% | ❌ Overestimates | OOF=0.9545 → LB=0.8269 (stacking) |

**Implication**: OOF metrics massively overestimate performance. Validation metrics are much more reliable for predicting LB scores.

**Why stacking failed**:
- OOF Mean AUC: 0.9545 (looked amazing!)
- LB Score: 0.8269 (reality check)
- Gap: -12.8% (massive overfitting to fold patterns)
